About

The Eye
Behind the Lens

I'm Nithin Sebastian, a landscape and nature photographer drawn to the edges of the world — places where the land is still untamed, where weather makes and breaks a scene in minutes, and where patience is the only real technique.

My work is rooted in long exposures, pre-dawn hikes, and the discipline of waiting for the light that changes everything. I work with a canon eos rebel sl3
